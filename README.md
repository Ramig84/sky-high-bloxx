# Sky High Bloxx

Build a playable modern Tower Bloxx game designed specifically for mobile phones (portrait 9:16 layout, touch-first one-thumb controls).

Recreate the classic Tower Bloxx / City Bloxx gameplay with modern upgrades and physics:
- Classic swinging crane mechanism with realistic pendulum swing carrying stylized apartment blocks.
- One-tap drop controls: tap anywhere on the screen to release the block onto the tower.
- Dynamic spring and wobble physics: off-center drops cause the entire tower stack to sway and bend, making subsequent drops harder to balance.
- Alignment feedback: 'Perfect' drop combo multiplier streak, combo audio chimes, celebratory confetti/balloon particle bursts, and little animated residents jumping into the windows.
- Altitude progression and dynamic camera: smooth vertical camera glide as the tower grows taller, progressing through street level, cloudy skies, golden sunset, and stratosphere.
- Modern 3D stylized art direction matching the attached concept image: colorful apartment modules with balconies, glowing windows, awnings, and vibrant city backdrop.
- Game loop: 3 missed drops (lives), score counter, combo meter, game over screen with high score saving, and instant replay button.
